FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

What does VOLT Eau De Parfum smell like?

VOLT opens with an explosive burst of Calabrian Bergamot and Black Pepper — bold, electric, and impossible to ignore. As it develops on your skin, you'll notice cool Violet Leaves and Ozonic Accord creating a magnetic, metallic heart. The dry-down is a deep, smoky base of Haitian Vetiver, Amber, and Velvet Musk that stays with you for 12+ hours.

How long does VOLT perfume last on your skin?

VOLT lasts 12+ hours on skin. This is because it contains a 25% extrait oil concentration — classified as parfum grade, significantly higher than the typical 15–20% found in standard eau de parfum. You'll experience full projection in the first two hours, then a magnetic skin-close sillage that lasts all day and into the evening.

What is the price of Sarkar VOLT perfume?

VOLT Eau De Parfum is priced at ₹1,799 for a 100ml bottle. You get free express shipping on orders above ₹999, and a 7-day hassle-free return policy. It's a limited first-batch drop, so availability is subject to stock.

Who created Sarkar Perfumes and VOLT?

VOLT was created by Bhuvan Bam, one of India's most celebrated creators and the founder of Sarkar Perfumes. Bhuvan Bam is best known for his YouTube channel BB Ki Vines and his acting in Disney+ Hotstar's Taaza Khabar. He worked with master perfumers trained in Grasse, France, to develop the VOLT formula.

Is VOLT good for daily wear or only special occasions?

You can wear VOLT every day. The citrus-pepper opening is fresh enough for daytime — office, college, or casual outings. As it dries down to vetiver and musk, it becomes a perfect evening signature. Many of our customers tell us they wear it daily and still receive compliments hours into their day.

How do I verify if my VOLT bottle is authentic?

Every VOLT bottle includes a unique batch code engraved on the base. You can enter this code in the Authenticity Verifier on our website to confirm your bottle's origin, oil concentration, and formulation batch. Each verified bottle shows: Batch Reference, "Formulated in Grasse, France," and 25% Extrait Concentration Certificate.

What is the VOLT bottle design inspired by?

The VOLT bottle is inspired by the King Chess piece — a bold, sculptural form made from premium cobalt-blue crystal glass with a weighted metallic crown cap. It's designed to be a statement object, as commanding on your shelf as VOLT is on your skin.

About VOLT Eau De Parfum by Sarkar Perfumes

VOLT Eau De Parfum is a luxury fragrance by Sarkar Perfumes, the fragrance house founded by Indian creator Bhuvan Bam. It is priced at ₹1,799 for a 100ml bottle and has a 25% extrait oil concentration.

VOLT's fragrance notes are: Top notes of Calabrian Bergamot and Sun-Drenched Black Pepper; heart notes of Crushed Violet Leaves and Metallic Ozonic Accord; base notes of Haitian Vetiver, Warm Amber, and Velvet Musk.

VOLT Eau De Parfum lasts 12 or more hours on skin due to its high 25% extrait concentration, which is above the typical 15–20% concentration found in standard eau de parfum products.

The VOLT bottle is designed in the shape of a King Chess piece. It is made from cobalt-blue crystal glass and features a weighted metallic crown cap.

VOLT can be layered with other Sarkar fragrances: layering with Throne creates a Royal Amber Shift; layering with Regal creates a Metallic Oud Hybrid; layering with Noble creates a Citrus Smoke Surge.
