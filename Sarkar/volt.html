PRODUCT DESIGN & OLFACTORY ARCHITECTURE CASE STUDY

VOLT — A Study in Fragrance and Identity

An avant-garde exploration in scent geometry: high-voltage Calabrian citrus meets ozone-charged violet leaf and smoky Haitian vetiver, housed inside an imposing crystal King Chess flacon.

Figure 1.0: Sculptural flacon render in deep cobalt-blue crystal glass with weighted metallic cross-finial crown cap.

1. Creative Inspiration & Olfactory Narrative

The VOLT concept originated from an investigation into contrasting sensory tensions: the clash between high-voltage modern velocity and ancient, deliberate botanical extraction. Traditional luxury perfumes often lean either strictly classical (heavy woods, powdery florality) or aggressively synthetic (fresh commercial aquatics). VOLT was conceptualized to bridge that dichotomy.

The fragrance narrative is structured around the metaphor of atmospheric electricity before a nocturnal storm in a subtropical capital: the sharp crispness of ionized air, ozone rising from asphalt, tempered by ancient roots clinging to earth.

2. Conceptual Olfactory Architecture (The Three Acts)

Rather than following a standard linear scent decay, the VOLT formula was theoretically designed as a three-act evolving sequence:

ACT 01 • THE FLASH (TOP ACCORD)

Calabrian Bergamot & Sun-Drenched Black Pepper

An immediate burst of high-acidity cold-pressed citrus from southern Italy, amplified by freshly cracked Indonesian black pepper. Designed to evoke kinetic movement and immediate sensory arousal.

ACT 02 • THE CHARGE (HEART ACCORD)

Crushed Violet Leaves & Metallic Ozonic Accord

As the volatile citrus top recedes, a cold, mineral green accord takes precedence. Crushed violet leaves provide a botanical bitterness, fused with an ozonic accord mimicking charged thunderstorm air.

ACT 03 • THE GROUND (BASE ACCORD)

Smoky Haitian Vetiver, Warm Amber & Velvet Musk

The foundation is anchored by wild-harvested Haitian vetiver roots, hydro-distilled to emphasize dry smokiness over damp peat. Luminous fossilized amber resin and clean velvet musk form a tactile, magnetic skin-scent aura.

3. Flacon Sculpture & Packaging Design System

The bottle design borrows semiotic language from the King Chess Piece — the ultimate symbol of quiet strategic command. Key industrial design considerations included:

MATERIAL & WEIGHT
Cobalt-Blue Hand-Polished Crystal

High-refractive optical glass with deep cobalt pigment tinting. Thick 18mm base weight engineered for balance, stability, and tactile luxury presence.

CROWN CAP FINIAL
Die-Cast Weighted Zinc Alloy

Custom-tooled cross-finial cap weighing 92 grams, featuring a precision magnetic click closure with laser-etched concentric alignment grooves.

ATOMIZER SPRAY RATIO
Ultra-Fine Micro-Mist Delivery

Designed around a 0.08ml metering valve producing an expansive 60-degree aerosolized plume that coats skin evenly without localized liquid pooling.
