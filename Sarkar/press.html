INDEPENDENT ACADEMIC & PORTFOLIO DISCLOSURE

An independent fictional fragrance brand concept developed as an academic and creative portfolio project. SARKAR / VOLT is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any commercial fragrance brand or public figure. All media assets shown on this page represent original portfolio design work.

1. Project Concept Release Notes

EXHIBITION NOTE 01 • BRAND POSITIONING

Reimagining Luxury Indian Fragrance Archetypes

An exploration into modernizing luxury fragrance storytelling. SARKAR / VOLT moves away from generic commercial clichés toward an aggressive, kinetic aesthetic centered on electric energy, architectural glass flacons, and contrasting olfactory geometry.

EXHIBITION NOTE 02 • PACKAGING DESIGN

The King Chess Piece Crystal Flacon

Detailed design documentation of the 100ml cobalt-blue glass flacon. Features an 18mm heavy crystal base, weighted die-cast zinc cross finial cap, and laser-engraved minimal typographic emblem.

EXHIBITION NOTE 03 • DIGITAL CRAFT

Zero-Render-Blocking Web Performance Architecture

Demonstrates that complex interactive luxury storytelling (dynamic canvas particle simulation, sillage timeline sliders, 3D bottle tilt) can be built on pure vanilla web standards with perfect Core Web Vitals and zero main-thread blocking.
